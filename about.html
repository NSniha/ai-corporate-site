About Aurelis

Clarity when the stakes are high.

We are an independent, senior-led advisory firm working with boards, CEOs and executive teams on the strategic and organizational questions that shape long-term enterprise value.

Our story

Built to stay close to the problem.

Aurelis was founded on a simple conviction: the highest-value advisory work happens when experienced people remain deeply involved from framing to execution.

2009 — An independent model

We began as a small group of former operators and advisers who wanted to combine rigorous strategic thinking with the practicality of people who had actually carried P&Ls, built teams and delivered transformation.

2016 — Global scale, without layers

As clients expanded internationally, so did we—but not by adding hierarchy. We built a networked partnership model that assembles senior expertise around each question, while keeping accountability clear.

Today — A focused firm for consequential work

Our work spans strategy, transformation, digital, AI, organization and performance. The common thread is a leadership team facing an inflection point where quality of judgment matters more than volume of slides.

How we are different

Senior-led. Context-specific. Intentionally small.

Our teams are designed around the problem, not around utilization. A partner stays involved. Specialists join when useful. The client team participates from the start so the solution is understood, owned and executable.
